A little more clarity

Good questions.

The details that make an everyday product feel easy.

What is spray-on lotion?

A body moisturizer in a quick spray format. Spray it onto your skin, smooth it over, and get on with your day.

How do I use it?

Spray onto your body and smooth it in, especially after a shower or whenever your skin feels dry. Avoid spraying toward your face.

Why sea kelp?

Sea kelp extract has naturally occurring water-binding compounds. We chose it for a lightweight, comfortable moisturizing routine.

Will it feel greasy or sticky?

It is designed for a comfortable, non-greasy finish.

Is it scented? What skin types is it for?

It is planned as fragrance-free body care for a range of skin types. Individual skin reactions can vary; patch-test new products and stop use if irritation occurs.
